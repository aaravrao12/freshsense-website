Support

How can we help?

Questions, bugs or feedback: freshsenseai.home@gmail.com

How do I scan?
Open the app and tap the scan button, then take a photo of your fridge or produce drawer. FreshSense identifies what is in the photo and estimates how fresh each item is.
How accurate are freshness estimates?
Estimates are generated from a photo. Use your own judgment for food safety, and when in doubt, don't eat it.
Do I need an account?
No. FreshSense works without an account.
How do I delete my data?
All data is stored on your device, so deleting the app deletes it.
What is the FreshSense device?
A camera puck that mounts inside your fridge and watches it for you. First shipments are targeted for 2027. Reserve one here.

About

FreshSense is a camera that lives in your fridge, sees what is there, and tells you what to eat before it goes off. Built in Allen and Plano, Texas, by founder Aarav Rao.
