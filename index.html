The lineup

One brain. Any shape.

Four working prototypes, one camera board. Scroll to meet each one.

01 · Black square

The flat one.

Low profile for a fridge wall or the underside of a shelf. The lid lifts off, the board sits in a printed cradle, and the lens looks out through the centre.

02 · Red square

Same shell, your colour.

The shells are printed, so colour is a filament change, not a new mould. Red was the first one we tried. Inside it is the identical board and battery.

03 · White round

The one for white fridges.

A round puck that disappears against a white interior. The ring on the face is the magnet seat; the hole in the middle is the lens.

04 · Black round

The original.

The puck from the pilot. Six households, thirty days, 36% less produce thrown away. This is the shape going to the first production run.

Working prototypes, photographed on the bench. Production units will be injection-moulded.
