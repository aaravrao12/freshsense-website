Fresh Sense

★★★★★  (36% less waste · 30-day pilot)

It isn't about watching your fridge. It's about never throwing good food away again.

A magnetic camera puck that sits inside your fridge, sees the whole shelf through a 160° lens, and scores every item for freshness each day. You get an alert before anything spoils, a recipe to use it up, and a grocery list that fills itself in.

FreshSense

Made for
your kitchen

FreshSense works towards a kitchen where nothing good goes to waste. In a 30-day pilot with six households in Allen and Plano, Texas, produce waste fell by 36%, 91% of spoilage alerts were confirmed by an actual discard, and each household saved about £100 ($120) a month.

Built by founder Aarav Rao, recognised statewide at the Texas Junior Academy of Science and a finalist at CoCreate Pitch London 2026.
